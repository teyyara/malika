# Complete easter-egg guide — Loop 025

**Full spoilers.** This is the complete walkthrough for Zack, not a hint sheet to hand to Malika before she explores. The website's hidden progress exists only in the current page session: it is not written to a server, browser storage, or analytics. Reloading starts a fresh hunt. None of these secrets is required to read the birthday letter or finish the main heart game.

## 1. Hidden entrances to the Secret Arcade

There are several ways in. Any one is enough.

1. **The classic controller code:** on the page, press **Up, Up, Down, Down, Left, Right, Left, Right, B, A**. The password door appears.
2. **Spell her name:** type **MALIKA** on the keyboard. A starfall reveal appears, titled *Her name lit up the sky*. Press **Enter the Secret Arcade** in the reveal to open the password door.
3. **Call the kitten:** type **MIMI** on the keyboard. The password door appears; it no longer opens Memory Match directly.
4. **The heart in the header:** after leaving the welcome screen, tap the small heart inside the “for Malika” header mark **5 times within 7 seconds**.
5. **The ladybug trail:** in the garden, click a ladybug **4 times within 9 seconds**. Close each love-note popup before clicking again. A toast appears; use **Open Secret Arcade** to reach the password door.
6. The other hidden entrances are the moon wish, Mimi's fifth hello, the footer star, and the birthday-letter wax seal; each is detailed below.

The typed words are case-insensitive. When using the keyboard shortcuts, type on the page rather than inside a text field. To unlock the Secret Arcade, enter **iloveyou** in the password panel. The five games appear only after the correct password is accepted; the unlock lasts until the page is refreshed.

## 2. Small secrets in the welcome scene and garden

### The moon's birthday wish
On the **welcome screen**, tap the large moon **7 times within 9 seconds**. The reveal is titled *The moon kept a secret* and offers a private wish for Malika.

### Mimi's fifth hello
Pet any visible Mimi **5 times within 8 seconds**. Her fifth hello opens *Mimi's fifth hello*, with her animated paw parade and a tiny birthday dance. Mimi reacts to ordinary pets too, so the early taps are not wasted.

### The little footer star
After entering the garden (or another scene with the footer), click the small **✦ button at the bottom of the page 3 times within 7 seconds**. It opens *A tiny star said thank you*. The footer star is a real keyboard-focusable button too.

### Follow the ladybugs
Each interactive ladybug opens its own love note. To reveal the extra trail clue, click a ladybug **4 times within 9 seconds**, closing the note between taps; then press **Open Secret Arcade** in the toast.

### Trace the heart-shaped constellation
In the garden landscape, find the three small twinkling constellation stars and activate them in order: **left star → middle star → right star**. Mimi teases you if you skip ahead; a wrong order does not award progress. Completing all three traces the heart and opens the hidden wish, *A little more wonder*.

### Touch the pink flower
Tap the pink flower on the right side of the garden landscape. It opens the love note **Brave new chapters**.

### Mimi has bonus lines
Pet Mimi in the garden to hear her changing little replies. The reply text also reacts if you get impatient with the constellation and try to skip ahead. Those comments are playful feedback, not a timed challenge.

### Optional clue trail
Under the garden destinations, open **Need a tiny nudge?** for a gentle hint. Open **Give me a clearer clue** for the more explicit clue: the numbers **3, 4, 5, and 7**, pointing toward the constellation, ladybugs, Mimi/header heart, and moon/footer-star interactions. The clue trail intentionally stops before giving away the full arcade solutions.

## 3. Mimi's Little Heart Garden

Open **Heart game** in the header or the garden destination card, then choose **Let's find some hearts**.

- Collect **7 purple hearts**. There is no countdown; each heart counts once. Restart is available if you want a fresh run.
- Winning opens the *You found every heart…* celebration and unlocks the secret letter **A letter behind the stars** in **Love notes**. Open that newly unlocked card to read it.
- While the game is running, pet Mimi for a bonus secret compliment.
- The bonus ladybug gives you another little note. It never takes away a heart.

The core game and its notes remain available even if no arcade entrance is ever found.

## 4. The Love Collection and birthday letter

### The locked letter
The Love Collection contains the regular notes, birthday wishes, and imagined future dreams. The card **A letter behind the stars** starts locked. Complete the seven-heart game to unlock it, then revisit **Love notes** and open it.

### One last little surprise
In **Birthday letter**, press **One last little surprise…** under the letter. This opens an extra animated birthday wish with a glowing portal, hearts, Mimi, and a ladybug.

### NEW — the wax-seal secret
Stay on the **Birthday letter** scene and tap the purple wax-seal heart at the top of Zack's letter **3 times within 7 seconds**. It opens a hidden note directly beneath the letter. The seal is a keyboard-accessible button; focus it and press Enter or Space for each tap.

## 5. All five Secret Arcade quests — full solutions

Enter through any hidden entrance above. Each quest has its own full-screen victory celebration. Decoys and wrong answers give feedback but do not permanently block the game.

### Quest 1 — Mimi's Constellation Chase
Find the eight stars that trace a heart and click them in this order:

**left outer curve → upper-left peak → inner-left curve → centre notch → inner-right curve → upper-right peak → right outer curve → bottom point.**

There are also four decoy stars. Avoid those and do not skip ahead in the heart trail. If progress stays at 0, start with the left-side star rather than a decoy.

Reward: *You found the heart in the stars.*

### Quest 2 — Mimi's Memory Match
Turn over two cards at a time and match all **four pairs**: **🌙 moon, 🐾 pawprints, 💜 purple heart, and ✨ sparkle**. The cards shuffle whenever a new round starts, so pair positions change. Memorize revealed positions; an incorrect pair briefly stays visible and then turns back. You can keep playing after a mismatch.

Typing **MIMI** opens the Secret Arcade door. Enter **iloveyou** if it is still locked, then choose Memory Match from the game list; this keyboard shortcut no longer bypasses the password gate.

Reward: *You remembered every little wonder.*

### Quest 3 — Pawprint Rhythm
Press **Watch Mimi's rhythm** and listen to/watch the six symbols one at a time. Then tap this exact sequence:

**🐾 pawprints → 🌙 moon → 💜 purple heart → ✨ sparkle → 🐾 pawprints → 🌙 moon**

The last two beats echo the first pair. A wrong beat resets the combo, but you can replay the cue whenever you need it.

Reward: *Mimi's tiny concert is a HIT!*

### Quest 4 — Firefly Hunt
Catch the **five bright pearl-white fireflies** and leave the three dim violet decoys alone. Their positions are: upper-left side, near the top just left of centre, right-middle, lower-right of centre, and lower-left. A dim violet light is a decoy; clicking it gives a teasing message but does not erase progress.

Reward: *You just lit up the whole garden.*

### Quest 5 — The Little Wish Jar
Solve the five riddles by picking the correct symbol in order:

| Riddle clue | Choose |
|---|---|
| Borrows sunlight, changes shape through the month | 🌙 Moon |
| Mimi leaves tiny stamps wherever she goes | 🐾 Pawprints |
| You can give it away and still have more to give | 💜 Purple heart |
| Twinkles in the dark but will not burn your fingers | ✦ Star |
| A wish with a glittering trail through the night sky | ✨ Shooting star |

Wrong symbols reveal a hint and do not reset the wishes already solved.

Reward: *Five wishes. One very bright future.*

### The grand secret — Keeper of Little Wonders
Finish **all five** arcade quests. Reopen the Secret Arcade and press **Open the grand secret**, which only appears after all five are complete. This reveals the full-screen *Keeper of Little Wonders* finale: a layered heart, nine comet trails, 112 flying symbols, and five treasures.

## 6. Loop 017 — The little life layer

These are new interactive touches across the birthday journey, not extra gates needed to finish it.

### Turn the tiny sounds on
Sound is **off by default**. Click **Sound off** in the upper-right on the welcome scene, or in the header when exploring the other scenes. This enables a small set of soft, locally synthesized chimes: Mimi's pet/dance notes, note-opening chimes, heart-collection pips, little sounds for correct puzzle moves, and a brighter victory melody. Click **Sound on** any time to hush it again.

The sounds are generated inside the browser with the Web Audio API. There are no audio files, recordings, network requests, external services, or autoplay; the toggle requires an explicit user action. Sound preference lives in page memory and resets when the page is reloaded.

### Make Mimi dance
Pet Mimi **three times within about 4 seconds**. She breaks into a little dance, wiggles her ears and tail, and gets a flurry of tiny heart/sparkle particles. Continue petting her: the existing **fifth hello** birthday secret is still there, so the extra dance does not replace the hidden paw parade.

Mimi also has more life when she is not being petted: she blinks, swishes her tail, and the happy-version Mimi in the birthday finale keeps up a gentle little bob. Browsers with reduced-motion preferences suppress the dance's decorative movement.

### Send a heart back from any love note
Open a note from a ladybug, the flower, or the Love Collection. Tap the **floating heart** beneath the note text. It bounces and glows, a small confirmation appears — *A little heart has found its way back to Zack. Mimi approves.* — and an optional soft chime plays if sounds are enabled. Each newly opened note resets this tiny interaction so every note can receive its own heart.

### Little sounds and movement across the experience
- Finding a heart, solving a memory pair, entering a correct rhythm beat, catching a real firefly, or solving a wish makes a subtle success chime when sound is on.
- The main seven-heart game also plays a brighter victory melody when all hearts are collected.
- Secret reveals get a soft three-note shimmer; each finished arcade quest earns a short celebratory melody.
- Wrong puzzle choices get a lower, playful “Mimi caught you” cue. The game mechanics and progress are unchanged by sound.
- During an arcade victory, tap the glowing **A LITTLE WIN FOR YOU** prize medallion to replay the victory melody without closing the celebration.
- The letter's seal, the floating hearts, the prize medallions, the garden flowers, the love-note title, and the animated finale still work with sound off.

Everything remains client-side, optional, and temporary. No new dependencies, paid service, remote audio, persistence, analytics, or backend were added.

## 7. Loop 018 — Mimi's voice, the love-letter soundtrack, and earned rewards

Loop 018 gives the experience a clearer sound identity. At the time of Loop 018, sound began **off by default**. Loop 024 changes the current behavior: sound starts **on by default**, and the welcome CTA unlocks playback with the first deliberate tap, so there is no separate toggle tap before you can hear Mimi. The same toggle immediately hushes both short effects and the love-letter music.

### Mimi finally sounds like a kitten
With sound enabled, tap Mimi. She makes a short, layered, rising-and-falling **synthesized cat-like meow**, and her tiny mouth opens and closes in time with it. Three quick pets still start her dance; that has its own playful tune and a little meow as the dance settles in. These are designed sound effects generated by the browser, not a real cat recording.

### Open a love note to hear its little soundtrack
With sound enabled, open any love note from the flower, a ladybug, or the Love Collection. A soft, looping romantic theme begins under the letter: warm sustained chords, a gentle arpeggio, and a small melody. A tiny animated music indicator appears in the corner, and the note's halo/orbits slowly glow and turn with the score.

The theme only runs while a note is open and sound is enabled. Closing the note or turning sounds off stops the music; turning sound back on while the note is still open starts it again. It does not autoplay on the welcome screen or play behind the rest of the site.

### Every achievement has its own little fanfare
The five arcade rewards now each use their own musical signature when the quest is completed and whenever the prize medallion is tapped again:

- **Mimi's Constellation Chase:** ascending, sparkling star fanfare.
- **Mimi's Memory Match:** a warm, layered chord and a bright final note.
- **Pawprint Rhythm:** a six-beat mini victory riff with a clear finishing chime.
- **Firefly Hunt:** high twinkles that rise into a glowing flourish.
- **The Little Wish Jar:** a warmer wish-like sequence that builds toward a soft high note.
- The **seven-heart garden game** and the **Keeper of Little Wonders grand secret** have their own fuller, longer celebratory fanfares too.

Each prize medallion identifies its matching sound in the page markup, responds to hover/focus/tap, and can replay the fanfare without dismissing the victory. The existing ambient sparkles, rings, reward particles, and per-game colour themes remain part of the celebration.

### Local, optional, and easy to stop
The meow, romantic score, and fanfares are synthesized in-browser with the Web Audio API. No music track, recorded sound asset, audio CDN, remote request, extra package, paid service, or backend is added. Everything remains temporary in page memory. Browsers with reduced-motion preferences suppress the new decorative mouth/indicator/halo animation while preserving the actual interaction and sound control.

**Browser regression checks:** `e2e/little-life.spec.js` verifies Mimi's animated meow, that opening a note schedules the layered soundtrack and closing it stops scheduled notes, and that a quest's prize points to its specific fanfare. The checks observe Web Audio oscillator scheduling, not a human judgment of sound quality or a physical speaker-level listening test.

## 8. Loop 019 — Real cat sounds, warmer music, and a better sound experience

This loop replaces the simulated cat voice from Loop 018 with real recorded cat sounds bundled alongside the site.

### Give Mimi a real meow
1. Sound is **on by default**. Tap **Open your birthday surprise** once to unlock browser audio; the **Sound on** control in the upper-right corner is there to hush it whenever you need.
2. Tap Mimi once. She now plays a real cat meow while her mouth opens and closes with a matching head movement.
3. Tap her a second time within the quick-pet window. She responds with a recorded cat purr, and her head/tail show a gentle purring vibration.
4. Tap Mimi three times quickly to trigger the familiar dance. The dance keeps its own light melody and includes a real meow.

Two different meow recordings alternate for a little natural variety. The purr is trimmed to a short section so it does not drone over the page. Muting sound stops currently playing cat samples and tones; hiding the browser tab quiets them too.

This keeps playback reliable and avoids depending on a third-party sound service. The source pages and attribution are listed in [docs/audio-credits.md](audio-credits.md): DRAGON-STUDIO's **Cute Cat Meow**, **Cat Meow**, and **Purring Cat** on Pixabay, each labeled for use under the Pixabay Content License.

### More musical warmth
The love-note score and achievement fanfares remain original Web Audio arrangements. Their musical layers now have a slightly fuller level and gentle echo, helping the romantic pads, arpeggios, and prize melodies feel less dry. The love-note soundtrack still runs only while a note is open and sound is enabled, and stops when the note closes or sound is muted.

**Regression coverage:** e2e/little-life.spec.js verifies that all three audio files load successfully from the site's own origin, a real meow buffer plays, the second pet plays the purr recording, and the mute control interrupts active playback. ## 9. Loop 020 — A more natural little soundscape

This refinement keeps the recorded cat sounds from Loop 019 and focuses on timing, layering, and the movement that accompanies Mimi's voice.

### Let the cat recordings sound like recordings
- Mimi's two meows still alternate between the locally bundled recordings; playback-rate variation is deliberately small so a real cat's call is not turned into a cartoon voice by heavy pitch shifting.
- A second quick pet gives Mimi a **3.2-second purr passage** from the bundled purr recording. A short fade-in and longer fade-out keep the beginning and ending softer.
- A new pet gently fades the previous animal voice. If a sound file is still decoding, only the newest pet request is allowed to play; rapid clicks no longer queue delayed meows and purrs over one another.
- Mimi's meow-mouth stays animated for roughly the voice clip's length, while her purr adds a quiet head tremble, tail sway, and warm cheek glow. Reduced-motion settings suppress these ornamental movements without disabling sound.

### Give the love song and rewards more body
The love-note soundtrack now layers softly filtered chord pads with gentle upper harmonics, a more rounded arpeggio, a melodic line, subtle left/right placement, and the existing short echo. The extra layers remain quiet under the letter rather than competing with its text. Prize chimes keep their individual motifs, while note music remains limited to the time a love note is open and sound is enabled.

### What the automated checks can and cannot prove
The browser suite now verifies that the recorded purr is scheduled for about 3.2 seconds and that rapid petting during delayed audio decoding plays only the latest cat voice. It also continues to check local asset loading, muting, romantic-score lifecycle, and the five prize-sound mapping. These are playback and interaction checks—not a human listening test of tone quality on every speaker or device.

## 10. Loop 021 — Mimi's sound mixer

The small **Mixer** control sits beside the Sound on/off button. It gives you a way to hear Mimi's actual bundled recordings directly, without hunting for her on the page first.

### Adjust the sound level
1. Leave **Sound on** enabled (the default). Your first deliberate welcome tap unlocks browser audio.
2. Open **Mixer** next to the sound toggle.
3. Move **Overall volume** to a comfortable level. It controls all locally generated music, reward cues, echo, and recorded cat audio together; the change is smoothly ramped rather than snapped, so it will not click harshly.
4. The selected level remains in memory while you explore this page, but resets when you reload. No browser storage or personal setting is written anywhere.

### Audition Mimi
Inside the mixer, choose **Hear a meow** for one of the real bundled cat calls, or **Hear a purr** for Mimi's longer purr sample. Each audition uses the same audio playback path as tapping Mimi herself, including gentle fades and the protection against stacking delayed sounds. Both preview buttons are disabled when the main sound toggle is off.

### What is verified
The new browser journey opens the mixer, changes the master volume and checks the Web Audio gain target, plays a locally bundled meow and purr through the audition buttons, and confirms all three files load from the site's own origin. The existing 36-journey suite continues to cover love-note music, mute behavior, prize fanfares, mobile layout, keyboard interactions, and the hidden games.

## 11. Loop 022 — A layered little soundstage

The **Mixer** now has separate faders underneath the overall volume control. These let you tune Mimi and the soundtrack independently for your own speakers rather than making every sound louder or quieter together.

### Four independent levels
- **Overall volume** is the master level for the entire site.
- **Mimi's voice** controls the two recorded meows and her recorded purr only.
- **Love-note music** controls the warm looping score that plays while a love-note dialog is open.
- **Games and little sounds** controls discovery pips, dance notes, button feedback, and each game's achievement fanfare.

The levels change smoothly to avoid abrupt clicks. Muting still stops all active sounds immediately; the fader settings remain in page memory so sound can be turned back on at the same mix. Reloading the page resets all levels. Nothing is saved to browser storage or sent to a server.

### Verify the separate mix buses
1. Turn sound on and open **Mixer**.
2. Open the flower's **Brave new chapters** note, then move **Love-note music** down a little. The soundtrack changes without reducing Mimi's voice or game effects.
3. Use **Hear a meow** or **Hear a purr**, then move **Mimi's voice**. Only the recorded cat clips use this fader.
4. Move **Games and little sounds** to change reward and discovery sounds while the overall level and cat channel stay independent.

**Automated coverage:** the browser suite instruments the Web Audio graph, checks that music, Mimi, and effects are routed through distinct gain nodes, and confirms each channel fader changes its own gain. It also covers the master fader, recorded meow/purr audition controls, soundtrack lifecycle, quick-pet race protection, and muting. ## 12. Loop 023 — Mimi listens to her own voice

Mimi's face now follows the **actual recorded meow or purr waveform envelope**. This is no longer just an animation timer that happens to last as long as the sound.

### See her voice come through
1. Turn **Sound off** on to enable the recordings.
2. Pet Mimi once. Her little mouth opens more on louder portions of the real meow and relaxes during quieter gaps. A soft lavender-pink halo and tiny head tilt respond to the same live sound level.
3. Pet her again for the recorded purr. The mouth stays quiet, while tiny head motion and a warmer halo breathe with the measured purr level.
4. When the clip finishes, the audio meter stops, the glow fades out, and her face settles back to neutral. Starting another pet cancels the previous visual meter alongside the previous audio voice.

The browser reads a small time-domain sample from an **AnalyserNode** attached to the very same locally bundled sound that is playing. The analyser is in the cat's audio path and is transparent to the sound; the voice/music/effects mixer still determines the audible level. No microphone, recording, speech-recognition service, or network audio endpoint is used.

The analyser loop exists only while a cat recording is active, and is cancelled on the clip end, mute, or tab hide. Reduced-motion preference disables the audio-reactive face movement while preserving the recording itself and the sound/mute controls.

**Automated coverage:** a Chromium test observes the actual live meter values while Mimi meows, checks the mouth and halo variables react, and confirms both settle to neutral at clip end. This verifies the DOM/audio-meter lifecycle; it is not a listening-quality certification on a physical device.
